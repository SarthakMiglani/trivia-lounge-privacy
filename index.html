Privacy Policy: Trivia Lounge

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What the app collects. Trivia Lounge does not collect, transmit or share personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network services of its own.

What is stored on your device. Your game progress (level, XP, daily streak, streak freezes, which questions you have seen) and your sound setting are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Purchases. The optional Premium unlock is processed by Amazon through the Amazon Appstore. Trivia Lounge receives only whether the purchase is active. Amazon's own privacy notice applies to payment data.

Children. The app contains no user-generated content, chat or advertising. Questions are general-knowledge trivia.

Third-party content. Some questions are derived from Wikidata (CC0 public domain). Fonts: Unbounded and Outfit (SIL Open Font License). Icons: Phosphor Icons (MIT).
